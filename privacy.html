YaLoSabes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026.

YaLoSabes is a personal tool. The only person who connects a TikTok account to it is its creator.

Data used

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok account, to confirm that the right account is connected.
  • The access tokens that TikTok issues after authorization.
  • The video files that the creator chooses to send.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are stored in a file on the creator's own computer. Videos are sent to TikTok only when the creator asks for it.

Sharing

The data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising. TikTok processes the data it receives under its own privacy policy.

Deleting data and removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok account settings. Deleting the local token file removes the stored tokens from the computer.

Contact: through the YouTube channel @ya.lo.sabess.
